I am a researcher working at the intersection of **text-based financial economics**, **corporate governance**, **empirical asset pricing**, and **machine learning**. I build scalable data pipelines, extract structured insights from large unstructured datasets, and use both econometric and computational tools to study real-world financial behavior.

My research focuses on how information flows — especially textual information — shape corporate decisions and market reactions. I am particularly interested in CEO communication, sentiment analysis, governance structures, and applied machine learning in high-dimensional financial environments.

---

## Research Interests

- **Text-Based Financial Economics** — CEO communication, sentiment analysis, event extraction, and market narratives
- **Corporate Governance & Disclosure** — Governance structures, reporting quality, and value-relevant firm behaviors
- **Empirical Asset Pricing** — Linking fundamentals, text signals, and price formation
- **Machine Learning & NLP in Finance** — Large-scale text processing, dictionary- or model-based sentiment, representation learning
- **FinTech–Bank Partnerships & Intermediation** — Platform-based credit, digital financial infrastructure, and evolving market design

## Selected Projects

**CEO Interview Sentiment & Event Analysis** (INSEAD, Singapore)
: Developed an end-to-end pipeline for 3,957 CNBC interview transcripts (26M+ words). Extracted CEO-only speaking segments, applied the LM dictionary, and designed event detectors.

**American Option Pricing via LSMC**
: Implemented a refined Longstaff–Schwartz Monte Carlo model with Laguerre basis functions. Performed sensitivity analyses on volatility, discounting, and dividends.

**Algorithmic Trading: Fair Value Gap Strategy**
: Designed a multi-timeframe strategy for Nasdaq-100 futures. Achieved 6% ROI in two weeks in controlled backtesting with strict risk management.

**Mortgage Default Risk Modeling**
: Processed 5M+ Fannie Mae loans and benchmarked multiple ML models for RMBS default prediction. Delivered >92% accuracy.
