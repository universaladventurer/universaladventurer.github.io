I am a Ph.D. student in Finance at the National University of Singapore. Before joining NUS, I received an M.S. in Applied Mathematics from Columbia University and B.S. degrees in Mathematics and Economics from the University of Texas at Austin.

My research interests are in **corporate governance**, **asset pricing**, **industrial organization**, and **financial intermediation**. Methodologically, I work with large-scale text and administrative data, combining natural language processing with causal inference. I have contributed as a research assistant to projects on market concentration in EU public procurement and on corporate governance reform in Japan.

See the [Research](/research/) page for working papers, or my [CV](/cv/).
